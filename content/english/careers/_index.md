ladder_rules:
  - "**Annual reviews on the B ladder.** Level-up requires evidence of technical depth, project impact, and external sharing / documentation."
  - "**Switching to the M ladder requires B4 or higher** (Senior Engineer). The most common path is **B5 → M1**."
  - "**B and M level alignment:** M1 ≈ B4, M2 ≈ B5, M3 ≈ B6, M4 ≈ B7, M5 ≈ B8. Same level = same compensation range."
  - "**Managers can return to IC.** Switching back to the technical track maps to the equivalent B level — you are not locked in by the channel."
  - "**Compensation follows level, not track.** No incentive to do management just to get a raise — domain experts get rewarded staying experts."
  - "**Group / subsidiary two layers.** M1–M5 grow inside a subsidiary; M6+ operates at the Group level. Subsidiary GM (M5) is the gateway into Group-level leadership."
