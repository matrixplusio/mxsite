This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how **Bytematrix (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd.** ("we", "MatrixPlus") collects, uses, retains, and protects your personal information when you visit [matrixplus.io](https://matrixplus.io) or use our products and services.

We commit to collecting information on a **minimum-necessary** basis and to processing your data in a manner consistent with China's Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), the Cybersecurity Law, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. About us (the data controller)

- **Legal name**: Bytematrix (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd.
- **Brand**: MatrixPlus
- **Registered in**: Shenzhen, Guangdong, China
- **Contact**: [contact@matrixplus.io](mailto:contact@matrixplus.io)

## 2. What information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- When you contact us via the [contact page](/en/contact/) or email: **name, email, company, message**
- When you request demos, whitepapers, or resource downloads: **email, company, role** (where applicable)
- When you apply for partnership or commercial inquiry: **contact details, scenario description**

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

The website is hosted on our own VPS. The nginx / openresty access log retains:

- **IP address, User-Agent, referer, timestamp, request path**
- Used for security protection, abuse detection, compliance audit, and troubleshooting
- Retained for **at least 180 days** by default, then rotated out

### 2.3 What we do **not** collect

- We **do not use** third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Baidu Tongji, etc.)
- We **do not serve ads** and **do not use** advertising tracking cookies
- We **do not require** account registration to browse public content

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

1. **Responding to your inquiries** and business engagement
2. **Delivering materials you have requested** (whitepapers / datasheets / demos)
3. **Maintaining the security and availability** of our site and services
4. **Complying with legal obligations** (regulatory cooperation, lawful requests)
5. With your **explicit consent**, sending product updates and event news (unsubscribe at any time)

We **will not**:

- Sell or rent your information to third parties
- Use your inquiry content to train public AI models
- Use your information for direct marketing without your consent

## 4. Storage and retention

| Type | Storage | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Email correspondence | Company email system | As long as necessary for business, up to 2 years |
| Website access logs | VPS (Hong Kong, China) | At least 180 days |
| Resource-download records | Internal CRM | As long as necessary for business, up to 3 years |
| Commercial contracts and invoices | Internal finance system | As required by China's Accounting Archives regulation, ≥ 10 years |

## 5. Who can access your information

We **do not** share your personal information with external third parties, except:

- With your **explicit consent**
- As required by laws, regulators, or judicial bodies
- As necessary to deliver services you have requested, through **authorized processors** (e.g., email delivery, e-signature). We sign data-processing agreements with all such processors, requiring protection at no less a standard than this Policy.

Inside the company, only **roles with a need to know** (sales, customer success, technical support) can access your information when providing service to you, and they are bound by confidentiality.

## 6. Cross-border transfers

Our servers are located in **Hong Kong, China**. If you access this site from outside mainland China, your data will be transferred to and stored in Hong Kong. We apply security protections equivalent to those at the original collection point.

## 7. Your rights

Under PIPL / GDPR and equivalent regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Right to know and access**: learn what information we hold about you
- **Right to correction**: request correction of inaccurate information
- **Right to deletion**: request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to portability**: receive a copy of your information in a structured format
- **Right to withdraw consent**: revoke previously given consent
- **Right to lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority (CAC in China, EU DPAs, etc.)

To exercise any of these rights, email [contact@matrixplus.io](mailto:contact@matrixplus.io). We will respond within **15 business days**.

## 8. Cookies and local storage

This site uses minimal cookies / local storage, **for functional purposes only**:

- **Theme preference** (light/dark): a `theme` key in local storage
- **Language preference**: Hugo path prefix (no cookie needed)

We **do not** use advertising / tracking / analytics cookies.

## 10. Minors

This site and our services are **not directed at children under 14**. If we learn that a minor has provided personal information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

## 11. Data security

We apply the same engineering security standards we recommend to our customers to protect our own data:

- HTTPS everywhere (TLS 1.2+)
- Defense-in-depth and minimum-exposure posture across servers and operations infrastructure
- Multi-factor authentication on internal accounts
- Least-privilege access with periodic review
- Full audit trail and alerting on critical operations
- Regular security assessments, vulnerability remediation, and incident drills
